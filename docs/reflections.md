# Personal reflections

This page is reserved for the student's own synthesis after working through the
experiments and independent exercises. No personal observations or claims of
mastery have been supplied for this initial skeleton.

The eventual account should explain how the understanding of quantum states,
measurement, entanglement, and interference developed through the course. It
should connect that understanding to specific notebook results, including
surprises, failed assumptions, or discrepancies with the theoretical prediction.

A concluding discussion can consider which methods appear practically useful,
what the small simulations cannot establish, and which questions deserve further
study. References to particular figures or computations will make the reflection
an evidence-based academic account.
